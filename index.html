An elevator dispatching system in C++17.

Hey, we're a team of IIIT Vadodara students. Aerovator is an elevator dispatching system we built in C++17 for our Object-Oriented Programming course. It uses the LOOK algorithm to decide which car serves which call, modeling 3 lifts across 10 floors with emergency routing and live telemetry. You can test it in the 3D simulator or browse the source on GitHub.

03 / Scheduling

LOOK algorithm

Cars continue in their current travel direction until no calls remain ahead.

  • 1 Pick requests along current travel vector
  • 2 Service calls in ascending or descending order
  • 3 Reverse direction only when ahead queue is empty
